### FORM-FREE TEST vs the sqrt(N) exponent -0.500
A 3-4 point log-log fit is fragile (local slopes above are visibly non-constant), so the
primary claim is stated WITHOUT assuming a power law: count how many adjacent-rung local
exponents are SHALLOWER than -0.500 and sign-test them.
quantity   global fits (8 settings)   adjacent-rung local slopes
drift (weighted)    8/ 8 shallower  p=3.91e-03   18/20 shallower  p=2.01e-04
drift (equal-w)     8/ 8 shallower  p=3.91e-03   18/20 shallower  p=2.01e-04
|2p-1| raw          7/ 8 shallower  p=3.52e-02   14/20 shallower  p=5.77e-02
|2p-1| excess       7/ 8 shallower  p=3.52e-02   14/20 shallower  p=5.77e-02

split by window (adjacent-rung local slopes shallower than -0.500):
  drift (weighted)   STEADY   10/10   p=0.0010
  drift (weighted)   STARTUP   8/10   p=0.0547
  |2p-1| raw         STEADY    5/10   p=0.6230
  |2p-1| raw         STARTUP   9/10   p=0.0107

READING:
  drift  decays STRICTLY MORE SLOWLY than m^-1/2.  In STEADY every one of the 10 adjacent-rung
         exponents is shallower than -0.500 (p=9.8e-4); pooled over both windows 18/20
         (p=2.0e-4).  This survives the weighting fix, the exact-null fix, full n, and the
         switch from the 2-point endpoint drift to an OLS drift over the whole window.
  |2p-1| decays essentially EXACTLY like m^-1/2.  In STEADY the local exponents split 5/10
         either side of -0.500 (p=0.62).  It does NOT refute sqrt(N); it is consistent with it.
  WHY THEY DIFFER: |2p-1| carries its own binomial noise floor 2*null-1, which IS m^-1/2 to
         3 decimals by construction (fit b = -0.498 .. -0.500), and that floor is 45-84% of
         raw |2p-1| at every R18/C10 STEADY rung (17.9-90.3% across all cells).  An exponent
         near -1/2 for |2p-1| is therefore near-tautological and carries almost no evidence
         about the noise model.  Once the floor is subtracted, the excess exponent is
         -0.14 .. -0.51 with rung-level CIs so wide that NONE of the 8 excludes -0.500.

HONEST BOTTOM LINE
  The sqrt(N) refutation rests entirely on the DRIFT observable, not on the agreement
  observable.  Under the seed bootstrap the task specifies, all 8 corrected drift CIs exclude
  -0.500.  Under the conservative rung-level interval only 2 of 8 do (R18/C10 STEADY,
  R18/C100 STEADY -- the two 4-rung, 10-seed cells).  What is robust under every treatment is
  the DIRECTION and its consistency: drift is shallower than m^-1/2 in 8/8 settings and 18/20
  adjacent rungs.  The paper should claim the direction, report the interval it can defend,
  and state the |2p-1| ~ m^-0.47 coincidence explicitly rather than let a reader find it.
