# MASTER TABLE — every experiment the campaign ran

Compiled 1761 runs / 1175.8 GPU-hours (re-derived from results/all_runs.csv this cycle). Verdicts: CONFIRMED 32, REFUTED 26, OPEN 9, WITHDRAWN 3, DEAD 2, UNINTERPRETABLE 2 (74 rows total) — **counted by `analysis/c73_mastertable_check.py`, not estimated.** Of the 32 CONFIRMED, one is `CONFIRMED (T-B)` (a registered direction that came out against the headline) and one is `CONFIRMED, RESCOPED c73`. 30 fact-check corrections applied. **Brought current at cycle 73 after a three-cycle deferral; covers c66-c73.**

**Bottom line.** MetaOptimize does not beat a properly tuned non-meta baseline on CIFAR-10 and the gap does not close with budget — 1.46 pp behind SGD+cosine at 100 epochs and a flat 1.8-2.0 pp behind AdamW+cosine across config-matched 100/300/600-epoch cells — and every hierarchical-pooling fix the project funded (M0 shrink, M1 additive r, zpool) was refuted by its own controls, with the r-dial shown to be a meta-learning-rate knob rather than a pooling knob. **CYCLE 73 CHANGES THE HEADLINE.** The ACCURACY REVERSAL (nodewise minus layerwise goes -3.946 pp at epoch 10 with 0/7 seeds to +1.258 pp at epoch 40 with 7/7, ResNet18/CIFAR-10/ms=1e-3) was the campaign's best surviving accuracy result and was explicitly flagged as threatened by the unrun hz9 tuned-meta-step control. **hz9 HAS NOW RUN AND THE THREAT IS REALISED:** with each arm at its OWN optimal ms the trend over the same epoch-10-to-40 window runs the OTHER WAY (+3.444 -> +0.670, a change of -2.774 pp against the shared-ms +5.204 pp), and the epoch-10 sign is itself reversed. The reversal is a distance-from-optimum effect and may no longer be written as a granularity result without the 'at a shared ms=1e-3' qualifier. What survives is the MEASUREMENT line showing per-weight meta-gradients are correlated, short-range and peaked at the kernel scale, whose bridge to a partitioning prescription was itself measured and found negative by >= 2.71 decades.
