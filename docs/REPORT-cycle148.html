Hierarchical MetaOptimize/ ALICE · Leiden/ cycles 1–151/ CORRECTIONS 204

What 2,809 runs actually established

Step-size granularity in MetaOptimize, audited end to end. Twelve results hold. Nine died, including the one the project was named for. The strongest is that 1,536 of 11.2 million parameters carry the entire scalar-to-layerwise accuracy gap — and that it is those specific tensors, not merely three tensors at that depth. The gap now also reproduces on an architecture with no residual connections at all, and the rescue survives 2.5× the horizon losing one tenth of one percent. And as of today the isolation works on a second architecture too, picked out in advance from the probe records. What the campaign still cannot do is name the mechanism — and this report says plainly which sentences that rules out.

The headline, stated against us

CORRECTIONS 171 · 175

MetaOptimize does not beat a tuned plain baseline.

Nobody in this corpus had run a tuned non-meta baseline outside CIFAR-10. On CIFAR-100 it now exists, and the family sits 5.699 pp below tuned SGD — 18.80 SE, measured in batch — with every rung of the SGD learning-rate ladder, including the worst, beating the best MetaOptimize cell the corpus owns. On CIFAR-10 the deficit is 1.46 pp and does not close with budget.

That reframes the contribution rather than ending it.

The paper this campaign can honestly write is not “MetaOptimize wins.” It is a partition audit: within the method, how the step-size parameters are grouped is worth 25–47 pp, the grouping that matters is a handful of named tensors rather than a count, and the whole effect survives tuning each arm at its own optimum.

What this establishes, and what it still cannot.

Depth is not the operative variable — that leg of the confound is closed. But the carriers are also ranks 1, 2 and 3 of 62 by meta-gradient magnitude, and the best carrier-free triple that exists on this network still carries 308× less mass. So IDENTITY-OPERATIVE means “not any three matched layer4 BatchNorm parameters” — it does not separate tensor identity from term magnitude, and no batch on this architecture can. The horizon leg is still open; ciso2 carries it.

And it replicated.

ISO and ONE ran here with spec strings byte-identical to ciso1’s at disjoint seeds. The contrasts reproduce: D_ISO +46.93 → +46.69, D_ONE +41.4693 → +41.4747 — five thousandths of a point. First direct test of this campaign’s claim that batch, not seed, is the unit of replication, and it passed.

What the carriers have in common

figure 5 · CORRECTIONS 188.3, 189.2

ResNet-18 has exactly five 512-wide BatchNorm scales and all five sit in layer4. Three are the carriers. Reading them against BasicBlock.forward on the live model gives a sharp split — and reading them against the probe gives an equally sharp one, on a different axis.

carrier · output is a summand of a residual addnon-carrier · sits behind a ReLU, feeds no add

Two confounds, both stated before the arms were launched.

On a ResNet, “feeds a residual add” and “is the deepest, widest BatchNorm scale” are the same measurement seen twice — the campaign has never manipulated residual structure, which is why cvg1 runs an architecture with no residual additions at all. Separately, the best carrier-free triple that exists on this model still carries 225× less meta-gradient mass than the carriers, so cdep1 can rule out “any three matched layer4 BatchNorm parameters” but cannot separate tensor identity from term magnitude. No batch on this architecture can.

Not a delay — a regime

figure 7 · CORRECTIONS 190, 196

The obvious objection to the rescue was that it merely postponed the scalar collapse. At 100 epochs ISO’s coarse group was still descending toward the clamp, so the accuracy might have been borrowed from a group that had not yet frozen. Re-running the identical intervention to 250 epochs settles it. The 100-epoch column below is read in-run from the same twelve runs, so the two horizons are paired.

The registered verdict is not RESCUE-SURVIVES, and I am not going to write it as though it were.

The scorer returned UNRESOLVED-NOT-PINNED, because a freedom gate sits ahead of the primary bar in the branch map and ISO failed it. That gate measures dwell time at exactly −15.000, and ISO chatters: it makes ~140 departures after first touch and sits within 0.05 nats of the clamp on 92–94 % of records. Move the threshold by that 0.05 nats — a 5 % change in step size — and occupancy jumps to 0.93. ISO’s step size never exceeds 1.2× the floor; the genuinely free arm runs at 86–131× it. So the gate puts two arms whose step sizes differ hundredfold in the same bucket. That is a measured defect in the instrument, disclosed — and the fix is a successor scorer by registration, never an edit to this one.

Off ResNet for the first time

figure 6 · CORRECTIONS 189, 194

Until this week every one of the campaign’s runs was a ResNet. That was the objection every finding shared. VGG11_bn_c100 keeps convolutions and keeps BatchNorm, and has no residual addition anywhere — which matters because on a ResNet “feeds a residual add” and “is the deepest, widest BatchNorm scale” are the same measurement seen twice.

The scope caveat lifts for the gap. It does not lift for the isolation.

Two different claims, and they must not travel together. No isolation arm ran on VGG. And the probe records say the carrier set there has cardinality one — bn8.weight, 512 parameters, 0.0055 % of the model, carrying 0.58 of the meta-gradient mass and flipping the remainder sign on 1.0000 of pinned records, while the other three 512-wide BatchNorm scales flip nothing. What transfers is the shape of the mechanism — a small BatchNorm set carrying the vote, the large convolutions opposing it — not the set. That refutes the four-tensor arm registered in advance for VGG, before it was run.

The isolation, on a second architecture

figure 8 · CORRECTIONS 198, 203, 204

The previous section lifted the ResNet caveat for the gap and explicitly not for the isolation. cvi1 is the test of the isolation. VGG11_bn has four 512-wide BatchNorm scales; the scalar arm’s probe records nominated exactly one of them as the carrier, so the control could be an exact twin — something ResNet-18, with only two non-carrier scales against three carriers, could never offer.

What transfers, and what hardens into a limit.

Scope lifts — the isolation now exists on a non-residual family. The tensor list does not transfer: three carriers on ResNet, one on VGG; what carries over is the rule that nominates them. Magnitude hardens on both nets: bn8 carries 255× the meta-gradient mass of bn7, and a pure magnitude account predicts this outcome exactly as well. Horizon is unmeasured on VGG — ISO’s coarse group was still ~1.5 nats above the clamp at epoch 99 and descending.

The isolation
Upgraded this cycle from “not publishable as written”. Now a two-architecture identification result. Writable, verbatim: “On two BatchNorm convolutional networks trained on CIFAR-100 at a shared meta step size of 10⁻³ — ResNet18 and the non-residual VGG11_bn — giving its own step-size group to the tensor set nominated from the scalar arm’s pinned meta-update records recovers the entire in-batch scalar-to-layerwise gap at 100 epochs (1.019 and 1.018 of it), whereas isolating the same number of BatchNorm parameters of the same width and layer type at the same or adjacent depth recovers none of it; the nominated sets differ between the two networks, and on both they are also the largest contributors to the meta-update, so the result identifies which parameters decide the outcome, not why.”
 BatchNorm networks only. One dataset, one shared meta step size, so both rescues are lower bounds. VGG measured at 100 epochs only. The controls’ “none” is a floor reading. Still not writable: “these BatchNorm scales are the mechanism”.

One sentence.

The paper this corpus supports is a negative result with a sharp localisation attached: MetaOptimize loses to a tuned baseline by a margin that is large, replicated and horizon-stable; the granularity of its step-size partition matters enormously, on two architecture families; and the effect localises to a single cut and to a tiny parameter subset that can be predicted from the meta-update records on both families — whose mechanism the campaign has measured thoroughly and cannot yet name.
