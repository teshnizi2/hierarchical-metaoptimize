HEADROOM -- HOW LONG A BOX-FREE RUN CAN LAST (the addendum's number, re-measured)
================================================================================================================
  commit 13b22ed: 'the top coordinate climbs at 92-100pct of max Lion speed; there
  is no box-free configuration at this budget.'  That velocity is the STARTUP one.

  Headroom is reported as the WORST SEED, not the mean.  A mean of per-seed
  records-to-hit is a mean of RATIOS: one seed with a near-zero Q4 velocity sends
  it to infinity and hides the seed that is about to bind.  For 'can I afford N
  epochs' the only useful statistic is the seed that binds first.

  arm                     v_Q4/vmax (max)  final max  headroom  extra epochs (worst seed)
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  cl5 w    cU (R18)                 0.174     -1.984     1.984                       22.8
  cl5 node cU (R18)                 0.065     -3.313     3.313                      102.7
  cl5 lay  cU (R18)                -0.130     -5.527     5.527                        inf
  uc5 r10 w   (R10)                 0.345     -0.964     0.964                        5.6
  uc5 r34 w   (R34)                 0.074     -2.524     2.524                       68.1

  100 records = 1 epoch.  At ms=1e-3 the WORST R18 weightwise seed has ~22.8 epochs
  of ceiling headroom and the worst R10 seed has ~5.6.  So a 40-epoch box-free run at
  R18 -- 20 extra epochs against 22.8 of headroom -- is MARGINAL AT THE WORST SEED,
  not comfortable.  Say marginal.  The one thing that makes it fundable anyway is that
  this extrapolation is an UPPER BOUND on the climb: the velocity has fallen from 0.95
  vmax at startup to 0.17 in Q4 and is still falling, so the true headroom is longer
  than the linear projection.  That is an argument for running it WITH a box-free gate
  scored first (bl5's B0.3), not an argument that the gate is a formality.
  R10 is NOT fundable past ~26 epochs at any margin.
